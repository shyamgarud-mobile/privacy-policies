# Chikoo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 August 2026_

Chikoo is a baby monitor that runs entirely on your own Wi-Fi network.

## What we collect

Nothing.

Chikoo has no user accounts, no servers, and no analytics. We — the developers —
never receive any information about you or your use of the app.

## Camera and microphone

Chikoo uses your device's camera and microphone to send live video and audio from
one of your devices to another. This stream travels directly between your two
devices over your local network. It is not recorded, not stored, and is never
uploaded to us or to any third party.

The connection is encrypted in transit using DTLS-SRTP, the standard encryption
used by WebRTC.

Capture only runs during a monitoring session that you have started by pairing two
devices. While it is running, the device acting as the camera shows a permanent
notification, which can stop the session with one tap.

## Data stored on your device

Chikoo stores a small amount of information locally so it can reconnect to a device
you have already paired with: a pairing token and the network address of that
device. This never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

## Third parties

Chikoo contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking. It does
not use any software development kit that transmits information about you.

## Children

Chikoo is intended for use by adults — parents and caregivers. It is not directed at
children, and children are not its users.

## Not a medical device

Chikoo is a supervision aid. It does not detect breathing, movement, or any medical
condition, and it is not a substitute for adult supervision. Always follow safe
sleep guidance from your health provider.
